# Tourism Journal Characteristics

## Tourism Management (TM)

**Publisher**: Elsevier | **Impact Factor**: ~12.0 | **Typical Length**: 8000 words

TM is the highest-ranked journal dedicated to tourism management. It publishes quantitative and mixed-methods research on planning, management, marketing, and policy.

**What TM Values**:
1. Clear management implications for tourism practitioners
2. Rigorous quantitative methodology (econometrics, SEM, experiments)
3. Large, representative samples
4. Theoretical grounding in management or social science theory
5. Novelty of context or phenomenon

---

## Annals of Tourism Research (ATR)

**Publisher**: Elsevier | **Typical Length**: 9000 words

ATR is a social science journal focusing on the academic study of tourism. It values **theoretical contribution** above empirical novelty.

**What ATR Values**:
1. Theoretical advancement in understanding tourism as a social phenomenon
2. Methodological pluralism (qualitative, quantitative, conceptual)
3. Engagement with social science theory (sociology, anthropology, geography, economics)
4. Critical perspectives on tourism development

---

## Journal of Travel Research (JTR)

**Publisher**: SAGE | **Typical Length**: 8000 words

JTR focuses on traveler behavior, destination marketing, and the travel industry. Strongly quantitative.

**What JTR Values**:
1. Consumer/tourist behavior as the central focus
2. Rigorous quantitative methods
3. Practical implications for destination marketing organizations
4. Innovative data sources (big data, experiments, biometric)

---

## Hospitality Journals (IJHM, IJCHM)

| Journal | Focus | Methodology Preference |
|---------|-------|----------------------|
| **IJHM** | Hospitality operations, HR, service quality, strategy | Quantitative; some qualitative |
| **IJCHM** | Contemporary issues, practitioner-oriented, trends | Mixed; strong on practical relevance |

---

## Journal of Sustainable Tourism (JST)

**Publisher**: Taylor & Francis | **Focus**: Sustainability, ecotourism, community development, climate change

JST is the leading journal for sustainability in tourism. Papers must demonstrate clear contributions to **sustainability theory and practice**.
